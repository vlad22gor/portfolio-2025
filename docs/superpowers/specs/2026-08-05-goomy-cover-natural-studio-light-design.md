# GoomY cover: natural and studio light variants

## Goal

Create two local ImageGen preview variants from the user-approved composition reference. Preserve the realistic phone projection and GoomY interface while transferring the established muted-lime palette and smooth matte material language.

## Shared composition

- The attached composition reference is authoritative for the phone pose, projection, scale, framing, wall/floor relationship, and camera perspective.
- The high-resolution GoomY mockup is authoritative for hardware details and every screen/UI element.
- The environment uses one continuous muted olive-lime palette with smooth, finely textured matte surfaces.
- No additional props, small geometry, arches, decorative objects, or added text.

## Variant A: Natural light

- Warm directional daylight from the upper side.
- Soft organic foliage shadows with a readable but restrained light pattern.
- Natural contact shadow and subtle glass reflection consistent with the light direction.
- Bright enough for both light and dark portfolio themes without turning neon or washed out.

## Variant B: Studio light

- Large softbox-style key light from above and to the side with gentle fill.
- Clean illumination gradient, controlled highlights, and a precise soft contact shadow.
- No foliage shadows or decorative light spots.
- The same palette, material, composition, camera, and phone projection as Variant A.

## Locked invariants

- Do not redesign, recolor, rewrite, sharpen, blur, or simplify the phone screen.
- Preserve phone silhouette, perspective, position, size, Dynamic Island, bezel, controls, UI colors, typography, icons, sticker, food photos, and navigation.
- Avoid dirty glass halos, edge smears, shadows around Dynamic Island, warped hardware, or perspective drift.
- Generate preview files only. Do not upscale, composite, normalize to production dimensions, or replace the website asset before explicit approval.

## Review criteria

- Phone projection reads as equally realistic in both variants.
- The only intentional difference between variants is the lighting model.
- Background color and material feel consistent across both outputs and with the cover series.
- UI remains legible and visually unchanged.
